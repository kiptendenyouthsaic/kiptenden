Song Number: 57
Swahili Title: Naendea msalaba
English Title: I am coming to the cross

Verse 1.
Naendea Msalaba,
 Ni mnyonge na mpofu,
 Yapitayo naacha,
 Nipone Msalabani. 
 


  Nakutumaini tu,
    Ewe Mwana wa Mungu;
    Nainamia kwako;
    Niponye, mponya wangu.

------

Verse 2.
Nakulilia sana:
 Nalemewa na dhambi;
 Pole Yesu asema;
  “Nitazifuta zote”.

------

Verse 3.
Natoa vyote kwako,
 Nafasi nazo nguvu,
 Roho yangu na mwili,
 Viwe vyako milele.

------

Verse 4.
Kwa damu yake sasa,
 Nimegeuka roho,
 Nikaziacha tama
 Nimfuate Yesu tu.

------

Verse 5.
Yesu yuaja tena!
 Nimepevuka kwake,
 Kila chembe kamili;
 Msifuni yeye mponya!

------
