Song Number: 39
Swahili Title: Mwenye dhambi huna raha
English Title: Come, ye sinners, poor and wretched

Verse 1.
Mwenye dhambi huna raha,
 Sikiza nakusihi,
 Utapata msamaha
 Kwake Yesu Mwokozi!
 Njoo hima, njoo hima,
 Naye atafurahi.

------

Verse 2.
Yesu anakwita sana
 Naye yuko Mbinguni;
 Hofu ya kifo hapana
 Kwake ukiamini.
 Njoo hima, njoo hima,
 Utapata amani.

------

Verse 3.
Hatakwita siku zote;
 Ni ya sasa nafasi.
 Lete na uchafu wote,
 Kukawa haipasi.
 Njoo hima, njoo hima,
 Ni wokovu halisi.

------

Verse 4.
Uzima uko kwa Bwana
 Twae uzima hasa
 Bure unapatikana,
 Wokovu twae sasa!
 Njoo hima, njoo hima,
 Twae utakatifu.

------
