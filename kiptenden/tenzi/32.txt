Song Number: 32
Swahili Title: Yesu awakubali wakosa
English Title: Sinners, Jesus will receive

Verse 1.
Yesu awakubali,
 Wakosa, wahalifu,
 Wambieni wa mbali
 Habari ya wokovu. 
 


 Tangazeni kwa bidii,
     “Akubali wakosa!”
     Liwe neno dhahiri,
     “Akubali wakosa!”

------

Verse 2.
Awakubali  Bwana
 Neno lake amini
 Watu kila aina
 Waje kwake tengoni.

------

Verse 3.
Mimi ni safi moyo
 Na mbele ya sharia,
 Aliye safi roho
 Kwake ilitimia.

------

Verse 4.
Akubali wakosa;
 Nami anikubali;
 Alivyonitakasa
 Mbinguni niwasili.

------
