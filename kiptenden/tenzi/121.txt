Song Number: 121
Swahili Title: Liko lango moja wazi
English Title: The gate ajar for me

Verse 1.
Liko lango moja wazi,
 Ni lango la mbinguni;
 Na wote waingiao
 Watapata nafasi. 
 


   Lango ndiye Yesu Bwana
     Wote waingie kwake,
     Lango! Lango
     La Mbinguni ni wazi.

------

Verse 2.
Yesu ndiye lango hili,
 Hata sasa ni wazi,
 Kwa wakubwa na wadogo,
 Tajiri na maskini.

------

Verse 3.
Hili ni lango la raha,
 Ni lango la rehema;
 Kila mtu apitaye
 Hana majonzi tena.

------

Verse 4.
Tukipita lango hili
 Tutatua mizigo,
 Tuliochukua kwanza,
 Tutavikwa uzima.

------

Verse 5.
Hima ndugu tuingie
 Lango halijafungwa,
 Likifungwa mara moja
 Halitafunguliwa.

------
