Song Number: 33
Swahili Title: Dhambi ikikulemea
English Title: Come every soul by sin oppressed

Verse 1.
Dhambi ikikulemea,
 Kwa Bwana rehema;
 Hivi sasa tegemea
 Neno la salama. 
 


   Tegemea, tegemea,
         akwita sasa.
    Ni Mwokozi, ni
   Mwokozi;  amini sasa.

------

Verse 2.
Yesu amemwaga damu
 Ya nyingi baraka;
 Nawe sasa oga mumu
 Zioshwamo taka.

------

Verse 3.
Ni njia yeye hakika
 Hwongoza rahani;
 Usikawe kumshika,
 Uwe barakani.

------

Verse 4.
Karibu nawe wingie
 Mwetu safarini,
 Twende tukamwamkie,
 Milele Mbinguni.

------
