Song Number: 4
Swahili Title: Jina la Yesu, salamu!
English Title: All hail for the power of Jesus's name

Verse 1.
Jina la Yesu, Salamu!
 
Lisujudieni,
 
Ninyi Mbinguni, hukumu
 
Na enzi mpeni.

------

Verse 2.
Enzi na apewe kwetu,
 Watetea dini;
 Mkuzeni Bwana wenu,
 Na enzi mpeni.

------

Verse 3.
Enyi mbegu ya rehema,
 Nanyi msifuni;
 Mmeponywa kwa neema,
 Na enzi mpeni.

------

Verse 4.
Wenye dhambi kumbukeni
 Ya msalabani,
 Kwa furaha msifuni,
 Na enzi mpeni.

------

Verse 5.
Kila mtu duniani
 Msujudieni,
 Kote-kote msifuni,
 Na enzi mpeni.

------

Verse 6.
Sisi na wao pamoja
 Tu mumo sifani,
 Milele sifa ni moja,
 Ni “Enzi mpeni.”

------
