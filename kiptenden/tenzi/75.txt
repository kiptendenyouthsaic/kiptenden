Song Number: 75
Swahili Title: Waimba, sikizeni
English Title: Hark, the herald angels sing

Verse 1.
Waimba, sikizeni,
 Malaika Mbinguni;
 Wimbo wa tamu sana
 Wa pendo zake Bwana,
 “Duniani salama,
 Kwa wakosa rehema.”
 Sisi sote na twimbe
 Nao wale wajumbe;
 Waimba, sikizeni,
 Malaika mbinguni.

------

Verse 2.
Ndiye Bwana wa Mbingu,
 Tangu milele Mungu,
 Amezaliwa mwili,
 Mwana wa mwanamwali;
 Ametoka enzini
 Kuja ulimwenguni
 Mwokozi atufie,
 Ili tusipotee.
 Waimba, sikizeni,
 Malaika Mbinguni.

------

Verse 3.
Mtukufu wa amani
 Ametoka Mbinguni,
 Jua la haki, ndiye
 Atumulikiaye;
 Amejivua enzi,
 Alivyo na mapenzi,
 Ataka kutuponya,
 Kutuzalisha upya,
 Waimba sikizeni,
 Malaika Mbinguni.

------

Verse 4.
Njoo upesi, Bwana,
 Twakutamani sana;
 Kaa nasi, mwokozi
 Vita hatuviwezi;
 Vunja kichwa cha nyoka,
 Sura zako andika,
 Tufanane na wewe,
 Kwetu sifa upewe,
 Waimba, sikizeni,
 Malaika Mbinguni.

------
