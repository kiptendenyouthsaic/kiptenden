Song Number: 68
Swahili Title: Mungu msaada wetu
English Title: O God, our help in ages past

Verse 1.
Mungu msaada wetu
 Tangu myaka yote,
 Ndiwe tumaini letu
 La zamani zote.

------

Verse 2.
Kivuli cha kiti chako
 Ndiyo ngome yetu.
 Watosha mkono wako
 Ni ulinzi wetu.

------

Verse 3.
Kwanza havijakuwako
 Nchi na milima,
 Ndiwe Mungu; chini yako
 Twakaa salama.

------

Verse 4.
Na myaka elfu ni kama
 Siku moja kwako;
 Utanilinda daima
 Tu wenyeji wako.

------

Verse 5.
Binadamu huondoka,
 Mwisho hana kitu;
 Kama ndoto hutoweka
 Ndivyo hali yetu.

------

Verse 6.
Ila wewe Mungu wetu
 Ndiwe wa kudumu;
 Ndiwe bora, ngome yetu
 Twakaa dawamu.

------
