Song Number: 11
Swahili Title: Nina haja nawe
English Title: I need Thee every hour

Verse 1.
Nina haja nawe
 Kila saa;
 Hawezi mwingine
 Kunifaa.
 


Yesu, nakuhitaji
     Vivyo kila saa!
     Niwezeshe, Mwokozi,
     Nakujia.
