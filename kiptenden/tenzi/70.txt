Song Number: 70
Swahili Title: Yesu atuchunga
English Title: Jesus is our shepherd

Verse 1.
Yesu atuchunga,
 Mchunga wetu,
 Naye atufuta
 Machozi yetu;
 Mkononi mwake
 Hatuna hofu,
 Daima twapata
 Kwake wokovu.

------

Verse 2.
Yesu atuchunga
 Tumemjua,
 Na sauti yake
 Twaitambua;
 Naye akituonya
 Ni kwa upole,
 Tu kondoo zake
 Hata milele.

------

Verse 3.
Yesu atuchunga;
 Aliwafia
 Kondoo wakubwa
 Na wana pia;
 Kwamba awajua,
 Kondoo zake,
 Hutiwa alama
 Ya damu yake.

------

Verse 4.
Yesu atuchunga;
 Mikono yake
 Imewaambata
 Kondoo zake;
 Hapati wadhuru
 Adui yule,
 Yesu awalinda.

------
