Song Number: 41
Swahili Title: Yesu aliniita, “Njoo”
English Title: I heard the voice of Jesus say

Verse 1.
Yesu aliniita, “njoo,
 Raha iko kwangu,
 Kichwa chako ukilaze
 Kifuani mwangu.”
 Nilikwenda kwake mara,
 Sana nilichoka;
 Nikapata kwake raha,
 Na furaha tena.

------

Verse 2.
Yesu aliniita, “njoo,
 Kwangu kuna maji,
 Maji ya uzima, bure,
 Unywe uwe hai, ”
 Nilikwenda kwake mara
 Na maji nikanywa:
 Naishi kwake, na kiu
 Kamwe sina tena.

------

Verse 3.
Yesu aliniita, “njoo,
 Dunia ni giza,
 Ukinitazama nuru
 Takung’arizia.”
 Nilikwenda kwake mara
 Yeye jua langu,
 Ni kila wakati mwanga
 Safarini mwangu.

------
