Song Number: 53
Swahili Title: Wewe umechoka sana
English Title: Art thou weary, art thou languid?

Verse 1.
Wewe umechoka sana?
 Wataka raha?
 Kwake Yesu utapata furaha.

------

Verse 2.
Alama anazo yeye?
  Sasa! makovu ya mikono, na miguu,
 Na mbavu.

------

Verse 3.
Nacho kimivukwa taji
 Kichwa cha huba?
 Alivikwa taji kweli miiba.

------

Verse 4.
Huku nikimfuata nipate nini?
 Maonjo nje na ndani amani.

------

Verse 5.
Kwamba namwandama Bwana,
 Mwisho ni nini?
 Ni furaha na salama
 Mbinguni.

------
