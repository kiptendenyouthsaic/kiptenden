Song Number: 59
Swahili Title: Peleleza ndani yangu
English Title: Search me, O God, my actions try

Verse 1.
Peleleza ndani yangu,
 Iwe safi nia,
 Kwangu kama kwako Mungu,
  Idhihiri pia.

------

Verse 2.
Peleleza moyo wangu
 Uunifunulie,
 Yaliyomo ndani yangu
 Nami niyajue.

------

Verse 3.
Kwanza washe zako tambi,
 Kumefunga giza;
 Nijue ambavyo dhambi
 Ni la kuchukiza.

------

Verse 4.
Peleleza na mawazo
 Ni mbegu za mambo,
 Asili ya machukizo,
 Maumbuo – umbo.

------

Verse 5.
Zidi kuyapeleleza
 Katikati yangu
 Hata wishe nifundisha
 Udhaifu wangu.

------

Verse 6.
Hapo nikikwinamia
 Mbele zako, Mungu,
 Hakika nitakujua
 U mpenzi wangu.

------
