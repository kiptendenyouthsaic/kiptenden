Song Number: 95
Swahili Title: Hivi vita vimekoma
English Title: The strife is o'er

Verse 1.
Hivi vita vimekoma,
 Vimeshindwa na uzima,
 Na asifiwe daima,
 Bwana Yesu.

------

Verse 2.
Nguvu za kifo ni hizo,
 Ila hazina uwezo,
 Ndizo sasa avunjazo,
 Bwana Yesu.

------

Verse 3.
Siku tatu za huzuni,
 Kisha atoka ufuni,
 Asifiwe duniani,
 Bwana Yesu.

------

Verse 4.
Mapingu ameyavunja,
 Mbingu amezifungua,
 Nasi sasa twamwimbia,
 Bwana Yesu.

------

Verse 5.
Na ulivyopigwa wewe,
 Ni kwamba tufunguliwe,
 Milele na usifiwe,
 Bwana Yesu.
