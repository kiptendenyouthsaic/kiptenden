Song Number: 82
Swahili Title: Sioni haya kwa Bwana
English Title: At the cross

Verse 1.
Sioni haya kwa Bwana
 Kwake nitang’ara!
 Mti wake sitakana,
 Ni neno imara! 
 


Msalaba ndio asili ya mema,
     Nikatua mzigo hapo;
     Nina uzima, furaha daima,
     Njoni kafurahini papo.

------

Verse 2.
Kama kiti chake vivyo
 Ni yake ahadi,
 Alivyowekewa navyo
 Kamwe havirudi.

------

Verse 3.
Bwana wangu, tena Mungu
 Ndilo lake jina!
 Hataacha roho yangu
 Wala kunikana.

------

Verse 4.
Atakiri langu jina,
 Mbele za Babaye,
 Anipe pahali tena
 Mbinguni nikae.

------
