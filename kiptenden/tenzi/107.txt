Song Number: 107
Swahili Title: Ewe Baba wa Mbinguni
English Title: Heavenly father, hear our prayer

Verse 1.
Ewe, Baba wa Mbinguni,
 Usikie twombalo
 Hapa chini duniani,
 Na uwe radhi nalo
 


 Ee Mungu, bariki
     Ndugu hawa wawili!
     Ee Mungu, bariki
     Ndugu hawa wawili!

------

Verse 2.
Watu hawa mbele zako,
 Na mbele ya kanisa;
 Watimize Neno lako
 Wanakutana sasa.

------

Verse 3.
Kama Yesu na Kanisa,
 Ni mmoja, na hivyo,
 Watu hawawape sasa
 Wawe mmoja vivyo

------

Verse 4.
Walinde, Bwana, daima,
 Wabariki nyumbani
 Uwape nyingi salama
 Humu ulimwenguni.

------

Verse 5.
Wabariki, ewe Bwana,
 Watu hawa wawili,
 Wape upaji wa wana,
 Wafurahishwe kweli.

------

Verse 6.
Siku za duniani
 Zitakapopungua,
 Wape kurithi Mbinguni
 Mema ya kwendelea.

------
