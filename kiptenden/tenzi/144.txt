Song Number: 144
Swahili Title: Mbele ninaendelea
English Title: 

Verse 1.
Mbele ninaendelea,
Ninazidi kutembea;
Maombi uyasikie,
Ee Bwana, unipandishe.
 


  Ee, Bwana, uniinue,
  Kwa imani nisimame;
  Nipande milima yote,
  Ee Bwana,
  Unipandishe.

------

Verse 2.
Sina tamani nikae,
Mahali pa shaka, kamwe;
Hapo wengi wanakaa,
Kuendelea naomba
Ee, Bwana…..

------

Verse 3.
Nisikae duniani,
Ni mahali pa shetani;
Natazamia mbinguni,
Nitafika na amani.
Ee, Bwana….

------
