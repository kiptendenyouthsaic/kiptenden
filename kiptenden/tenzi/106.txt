Song Number: 106
Swahili Title: Mungu awe nanyi daima
English Title: God be with you till we meet again

Verse 1.
Mungu awe nanyi daima,
 Hata twonane ya pili,
 Awachunge kwa fadhili,
 Mungu awe nanyi daima. 
 


Hata twonane huko juu,
     Hata twonane kwake kwema;
     Hata twonane huko juu,
     Mungu awe nanyi daima.

------

Verse 2.
Mungu awe nanyi daima;
 Ziwafunike mbawaze,
 Awalishe, awakuze;
 Mungu awe nanyi daima.

------

Verse 3.
Mungu awe nanyi daima;
 Kila wakati wa shani
 Awalinde hifadhini;
 Mungu awe nanyi daima.

------

Verse 4.
Mungu awe nanyi daima;
 Awabarikie sana,
 Awapasulie kina;
 Mungu awe nanyi daima.

------
