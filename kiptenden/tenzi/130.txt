Song Number: 130
Swahili Title: Niongoze, Bwana Mungu
English Title: Guide me, o thou great Jehovah

Verse 1.
Niongoze, Bwana Mungu,
 Ni msafiri chini;
 Ni mnyonge, nguvu sina:
 Nishike mkononi;
 U mkate wa Mbinguni,
 nilishe siku zote.

------

Verse 2.
Kijito cha maji mema,
 Kitokacho mwambani,
 Nguzo yako, moto, wingu,
 Yaongoza jangwani;
 Niokoe mwenye nguvu;
 Nguvu zangu na ngao.

------

Verse 3.
Nikikaribia kufa,
 Sichi neno lolote,
 Wewe kifo umeshinda
 Zinawe nguvu zote,
 Tutaimba sifa zako,
 Kwako juu milele.

------
