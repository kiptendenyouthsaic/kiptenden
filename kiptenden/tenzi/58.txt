Song Number: 58
Swahili Title: Mwamba mwenye imara
English Title: Rock of ages, cleft for me

Verse 1.
Mwamba wenye imara
 Kwako nitajificha!
 Maji hayo na damu
 Yaliyotoka humu,
 Hunisafi na dhambi,
 Hunifanya mshindi.

------

Verse 2.
Kwa kazi zote pia ,
 Sitimizi sharia.
 Nijapofanya bidii,
 Nikilia nakudhii,
 Hayaishi makosa;
 Ndiwe wa kuokoa.

------

Verse 3.
Sina cha mkononi,
 Naja Msalabani;
 Nili tupu, nivike;
 Ni mnyonge, nishike;
 Nili mchafu naja,
 Nioshe nisijafa.

------

Verse 4.
Nikungojapo chini,
 Nakwenda kaburini;
 Nipaapo Mbinguni,
 Na kukwona enzini:
 Roho yangu na iwe
 Rahani mwako wewe.

------
