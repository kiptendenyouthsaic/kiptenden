Song Number: 74
Swahili Title: Wachunga walipolinda
English Title: While shepherds watched their flocks

Verse 1.
Wachunga walipolinda
 Kucha nyama zao,
 Malaika mtukufu
 Alishuka kwao.

------

Verse 2.
Wakacha sana wachunga,
 Akawatuliza,
  “Nawaletea habari
 Ya kuwapendeza.”

------

Verse 3.
”Mji ule wa Daudi
 Leo amezawa
 Mwokozi ni Kristo Bwana,
 Ilivyoandikwa”.

------

Verse 4.
”Huyo mwana wa Mbinguni
 Ataonekana,
 Amelazwa kihorini
 Malazi hapana.”

------

Verse 5.
Alipokwisha yanena
 Malaika hao
 Waliimba wimbo huu
 Usio na mwisho:

------

Verse 6.
”Enzi ni yake Munju juu,
 Na nchi salama,
 Kwa watu nao radhi kuu,
 Sasa na daima.”

------
