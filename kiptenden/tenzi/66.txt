Song Number: 66
Swahili Title: Po pote mashamba yajaa
English Title: Far and near the fields

Verse 1.
Po pote mashamba yajaa,
 Tele nafaka pevu,
 Po pote yang’aa meupe
 Bondeni na nyandani.


     Mwenye mavuno, wasihi
     Upeleke wavuni,
     Wakusanye mazao,
     Hata kazi iishe.

------

Verse 2.
Wapeleke uchaoni,
 Waende na jotoni,
 Mpaka jua lishukapo
 Wakusanye ko kote.

------

Verse 3.
Watenda kazi wa Bwana,
 Mlete sasa mazao,
 Na jioni ingieni
 Kwake na furaha kuu.

------
