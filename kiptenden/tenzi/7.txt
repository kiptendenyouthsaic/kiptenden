Song Number: 7
Swahili Title: Ni tabibu wa karibu
English Title: The great physician now is near

Verse 1.
Ni tabibu wa karibu;
 Tabibu wa ajabu;
 Na rehema za daima;
 Ni dawa yake njema.


    Imbeni, malaika,
      Sifa za Yesu Bwana;
      Pweke limetukuka
      Jina lake Yesu.

------

Verse 2.
Hatufai kuwa hai,
 Wala hatutumai,
 Ila kweli yeye ndiye
 Atupumzishaye.

------

Verse 3.
Dhambi pia na hatia
 Ametuchukulia;
 Twendeni na amani
 Hata kwake Mbinguni.

------

Verse 4.
Huliona tamu jina
 La Yesu Kristo Bwana,
 Yuna sifa mwenye kufa
 Asishindwe na kufa.

------

Verse 5.
Kila mume asimame,
 Sifa zake zivume;
 Wanawake na washike
 Kusifu jina lake.

------

Verse 6.
Na vijana wote tena,
 Wapendao sana
 Waje kwake wawe wake
 Kwa utumishi wake.

------
