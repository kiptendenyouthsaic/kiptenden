Song Number: 111
Swahili Title: Neno lako Bwana
English Title: Lord, thy word abideth

Verse 1.
Neno lako, Bwana,
 Ni imara sana;
 Lilo latwongoza,
 Lilo latufunza.

------

Verse 2.
Adui wabaya
 Wakikaribia,
 Neno lake Bwana
 Ni ulinzi sana.

------

Verse 3.
Siku za dhoruba
 Soma ukiomba;
 Neno lake Bwana
 Msaada sana.

------

Verse 4.
Ukiliamini,
 Hwenda na amani;
 Una na furaha
 Neno ni siraha.

------

Verse 5.
Ni furaha kweli,
 Na wingi wa mali,
 Neno lake Bwana
 Kwa wasiokana

------

Verse 6.
Neno la rehema,
 Tukali wazima;
 Faraja I papo,
 Tufarikanapo.

------
