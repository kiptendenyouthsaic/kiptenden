Song Number: 46
Swahili Title: Twae wangu uzima
English Title: Take my life, and let it be

Verse 1.
Twae wangu uzima,
 Sadaka ya daima;
 Twae saa na siku,
 Zikutukuze huku.

------

Verse 2.
Twae mikono nayo
 Ifanye upendavyo,
 Twae yangu miguu
 Kwa wongozi wako tu.

------

Verse 3.
Twae sauti yangu,
 Niimbe kwa Mungu tu;
 Itwae na midomo,
 Ijae neno lako.

------

Verse 4.
Twae dhanabu pia,
 Na yote ya dunia;
 Twae yangu hekima,
 Upendavyo tumia.

------

Verse 5.
Nia itwae, Mungu,
 Haitakuwa yangu;
 Twae moyo;  ni wako,
 Uwe makazi yako.

------

Verse 6.
Twae mapenzi yangu,
 Sifa za moyo wangu;
 Twae kabisa nafsi
 Nniwe wako halisi.

------
