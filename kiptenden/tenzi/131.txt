Song Number: 131
Swahili Title: Piga sana vita vyema
English Title: Fight the good fight

Verse 1.
Piga sana vita vyema
 Kwa ushujaa daima,
 Yesu ndiye nguvu zako,
 Yesu ndiye kweli yako.

------

Verse 2.
Kaza mwendo, ushindane
 Angaza macho, umwone

 Naye ndiye tuzo lako.

Verse 3.
Tupa kizito, simama,
 Tazama mbele, si nyuma;
 Ni yeye uzima wako,
 Naye ni kipendo chako.

------

Verse 4.
Tangamka, uamini
 Akushika mikononi
 Hageuki, akupenda,
