Song Number: 98
Swahili Title: Huyo ndiye anashuka
English Title: Lo, he comes with clouds descending

Verse 1.
Huyo ndiye! anashuka,
 Aliyetufilia,
 Wengi waliookoka,
 Wakimfurahia,
 Aleluya!
 Yesu aturudia.

------

Verse 2.
Sote tutamtazama,
 Amekaa kitini,
 Nao waliomcoma,
 Kumkaza mtini,
 Na kilio, wamuone enzini.

------

Verse 3.
Alama za kifo chake
 Hata sasa anazo
 Na waaminifu wake
 Wapendezewa nazo.
 Alipata,
 Kwetu alama hizo.

------

Verse 4.
Wokovu utakiwao
 Sasa wapatikana,
 Na watakatifu hao
 Mbinguni wakutana
 Kumlaki,
 Ndiyo siku ya Bwana

------

Verse 5.
Wakusujudie wote
 Mbele ya kiti chako.
 Zako, Bwana, nguvu zote
 Itwae milki yako
 Njoo Bwana,
 Sisi tu watu wako.
